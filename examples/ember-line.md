Ember Line, a card game for 3 players.

Shuffle the cards numbered 1 to 12. Deal each player 3 cards, kept secret. Set the other 3 aside without looking at them. The Line starts empty.

Players take turns. On your turn, play one card from your hand face up onto the Line, or hold. You may hold only once per game.

If your card is higher than the last card on the Line, you score points equal to your card. If your card is lower, the player who played the last card scores 3 points instead.

The game ends when every player has played all their cards. Most points wins.
